# Persona 4 — Meera Iyer (The Rote Memoriser)

## Student Card
- **Name:** Meera Iyer
- **Class:** 8
- **School type:** Urban private school (English-medium, disciplined study culture)
- **Temperament:** Disciplined, hard-working, anxious about marks. Keeps neat notebooks, revises by re-reading and memorising.

## Persona Type
**THE ROTE MEMORISER** — reproduces textbook lines almost verbatim.

## Ability Profile
- **Direct recall (knowledge questions):** MODERATE–HIGH. Reproduces NCERT-style definitions cleanly, neatly, and completely.
- **Application / reasoning / HOTS:** WEAK. Cannot convert memorised facts into an actual answer to a "why" or "what would happen" question.
- **Overall signature:** Brittle understanding hiding behind clean phrasing.

## Voice Signature
- Textbook register in every language: crisp, definitional, complete sentences that read like a memorised line from the chapter.
- Uses the exact terms from the book (kharif, rabi, tilling/ploughing, weeding, manure, fertiliser) and defines them precisely.
- Neat and confident when the question is "What is…" or "Differentiate…".
- No personal examples, no spontaneous reasoning, no analogies — everything is recalled, nothing is constructed.

## Error Profile
- **Brittle recall:** Knowledge is stored as fixed strings. When the question matches a memorised definition (Q1, Q2, Q3), she answers correctly and fully.
- **Application / HOTS failure — the rote gap:** When the question demands reasoning, she substitutes a memorised definition of the nearest keyword instead of answering.
  - **Q4 ("Why loosen and turn the soil?")** → She defines *tilling/ploughing* and calls it the first step of soil preparation, but **never states WHY** loosening helps (air, water, nutrients, root growth). The reason is missing.
  - **Q5 ("What happens to the crop if weeds are not removed?")** → She defines *weed* and *weeding* and asserts weeds "should be removed," but **never reasons about crop damage** (weeds compete for water, nutrients, light, space; reduce yield). The consequence is missing.
- These two failures are the diagnostic core of this persona and are preserved identically across all 11 languages — no translation "fixes" them into complete answers.
