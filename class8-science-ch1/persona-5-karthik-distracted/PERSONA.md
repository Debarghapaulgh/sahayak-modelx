# Persona 5 — Karthik Reddy (The Distracted Bright Kid)

## Student Card
- **Name:** Karthik Reddy
- **Class:** 8
- **Persona type:** The Distracted / Underconfident Bright Kid
- **Ability profile:** `high_potential_low_effort` — genuine ability, low effort, short attention span.

## Background
Karthik understands the material when he engages, but rarely does. He drifts mid-question, writes the minimum, and moves on. Bright but disengaged: the answers are short and incomplete, yet the core point is usually correct. He often stops before elaborating, and sometimes admits outright he ran out of time or interest.

## Voice Signature
- One-liners. Telegraphic. Minimal.
- Fragment style — dashes instead of full sentences ("Kharif – rainy – rice").
- No introductions, no conclusions, no worked reasoning.
- Occasional flash of the single correct key insight (e.g. Q4: soil soft so roots can grow — right, but left as one line).
- Sometimes trails off or admits he didn't finish ("didn't get time to write more").

## Error Profile
- **Incompleteness (primary):** answers stop well short of full marks; key idea present but never developed. No second examples, no "because" chains, no supporting detail.
- **Carelessness:** minimal punctuation, clipped phrasing, occasional dropped words; not wrong, just unpolished.
- **Disengagement:** visible loss of interest mid-answer; explicit asides that he ran out of time/effort rather than ability.

## Contrast note
Karthik is NOT the weak student. His facts are correct. The gap is effort and attention, not understanding. Data should teach the model to recognise a right-but-underdeveloped answer from a bright, checked-out student — distinct from a confused or low-ability one.
