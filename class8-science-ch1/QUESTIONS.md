# Class 8 Science — Chapter 1: Crop Production and Management
## Question set + ideal answer key (rubric)

Board: CBSE / NCERT · Class: VIII · Chapter 1 · Grade level: ~13 yrs
Source: standard NCERT Ch-1 question bank (no Class-8 fixture in repo; Class 9/10 only).

These 5 questions span the difficulty ladder used for the persona experiment:
knowledge → comprehension → misconception-prone → application → HOTS/reasoning.

---

### Q1 — [knowledge, 2 marks]
**What is agriculture? Name the two major crop seasons in India.**

**Ideal:** Agriculture is the practice of growing crops and rearing animals for food,
fodder, fibre and other needs. The two major crop seasons are **Kharif** (rainy season,
June–October) and **Rabi** (winter season, October–March).

---

### Q2 — [comprehension, 3 marks]
**Differentiate between kharif and rabi crops. Give one example of each.**

**Ideal:** Kharif crops are sown at the onset of the monsoon (June–July), harvested in
Sept–Oct, and need plenty of water — e.g. paddy, maize, cotton. Rabi crops are sown in
winter (Oct–Nov), harvested in March–April, and need less water — e.g. wheat, gram,
mustard. Difference lies in growing season and water requirement.

---

### Q3 — [comprehension, misconception-prone, 2 marks]
**What is the difference between manure and fertiliser?**

**Ideal:** Manure is a natural organic substance from decomposed plant/animal waste; it
adds humus, improves soil texture and water-holding capacity, but has fewer nutrients.
Fertiliser is a manufactured chemical rich in specific nutrients (N, P, K); it acts fast
but adds no humus and overuse harms soil fertility.

> Diagnostic: struggling learners routinely merge both into one word ("khaad"). Good
> signal question for the differentiation model.

---

### Q4 — [application, 3 marks]
**Why is it necessary to loosen and turn the soil (tilling/ploughing) before sowing seeds?**

**Ideal:** Tilling aerates the soil so roots can breathe, lets roots penetrate deeper,
brings nutrient-rich soil to the top, improves water absorption, and lets earthworms and
microbes thrive. Loose soil lets tender roots spread easily.

> Diagnostic: rote learners define "tilling" but never answer *why* — the application gap.

---

### Q5 — [HOTS / reasoning, 3 marks]
**What might happen to a crop if weeds are not removed in time? Give at least two reasons.**

**Ideal:** Weeds compete with the crop for water, mineral nutrients, sunlight and space,
so the crop grows weaker and yield falls. Some weeds are harmful and interfere with
harvesting. Hence timely weeding (manual or weedicide) is essential.

> Diagnostic: separates real reasoning from memorised definitions.
