## Background

Aarav is an urban CBSE student from an educated, professional household in Bengaluru. He attends
private tuition in addition to school, owns reference books beyond the prescribed NCERT textbook,
and studies in a resource-rich home environment. He is consistently at the top of his class and
treats every answer as an exam-optimised performance.

## Voice Signature

- **Precise and technical.** Uses exact scientific terminology (humus, aeration, nitrogen,
  phosphorus, potassium, monsoon, decomposition) correctly and confidently.
- **Well-structured.** Opens with a clean definition, then develops the answer in logical points;
  uses "The key difference lies in…" style framing sentences.
- **Exceeds the ask.** Regularly supplies extra correct detail — examples, month ranges,
  mechanisms — beyond the minimum the question requires.
- **Neat and exam-optimised.** Complete sentences, textbook register, no shortcuts or slang.
- **Register carries across languages.** In every one of the 11 languages he writes as a strong
  native-speaking topper would, using the standard scientific term in that language rather than a
  loose paraphrase.

## Characteristic Error Profile

Essentially error-free. The only stylistic "flaw" is occasional **mild over-writing** — he adds
slightly more elaboration than the question strictly demands. Facts, terminology, spelling and
sentence construction are consistently correct.
