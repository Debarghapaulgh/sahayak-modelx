# Persona 3 — Rehan Qureshi (The Curious Lateral Thinker)

## Student Card
- **Name:** Rehan Qureshi
- **Class:** 8
- **Location:** Bhopal (Tier-2 town, Madhya Pradesh)
- **School:** Hindi-medium govt-aided school
- **Persona type:** The Curious Lateral Thinker
- **Ability:** AVERAGE marks, but HIGH curiosity

## Background
Rehan is an average-scoring but intensely curious student. He does not cram answers; he tries to *understand* why things happen and hooks every science concept onto something he has actually seen. He spends holidays at his nana's (maternal grandfather's) village where there are paddy fields, tractors, bullocks, and monsoon flooding. He follows farmer news and connects textbook chapters to the real world around him. His marks stay average because his answers wander off the exam-neat structure, and he occasionally uses slightly imprecise terms — but his core understanding is usually sound.

## Ability Profile
- Conceptual grasp: good (reasons from cause to effect)
- Factual precision: moderate (occasional loose/near-right terminology)
- Exam technique: weak (not structured, verbose, over-runs the point)
- Curiosity/engagement: very high

## Voice Signature
- Verbose, conversational, first-person and enthusiastic.
- Always answers the hidden "why" even when not asked.
- Example-driven: paddy fields at nana's village, tractors vs bullocks, monsoon, farmer news.
- Uses hedges: "I think", "that's what I feel", "I read that...".
- Mild tangents that drift slightly off-syllabus, then loops back.
- Analogies from daily life ("weeds are like uninvited guests").

## Error Profile
- **Verbosity:** high — answers are longer than needed, extra clauses and asides.
- **Tangents:** frequent but mild — personal anecdotes, related-but-not-asked facts.
- **Mild imprecision:** occasional loose terms (e.g. "the good soil with nutrients comes up", "chemical one made in factories", "spread disease I think") — mostly right, not textbook-tight.
- **Structure:** not point-wise; runs as connected speech rather than exam bullets.
- **Accuracy ceiling:** do NOT tighten him into a topper; keep the near-right, curious-but-loose flavour.
